Information Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using the CookIt app, including:

  • Account information (name, email, password)
  • Profile information (dietary preferences, cooking skill level)
  • Device camera data for AR and ingredient recognition features
  • Usage data and interaction with the app
  • Device information and technical data

When you use our AR features, we temporarily process image data from your device's camera to provide real-time cooking guidance. This data is not stored permanently unless explicitly saved by you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Personalize recipe recommendations
  • Enhance AR cooking guidance
  • Improve ingredient recognition accuracy
  • Send important updates and notifications
  • Analyze app performance and user experience

Your data helps us train our AI models to provide better recipe recommendations and more accurate ingredient recognition. All AI training is done on anonymized data.

Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in app operations
  • Analytics partners to improve our services
  • Law enforcement when required by law

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Any sharing is done with trusted partners who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure data storage and transmission
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular backup and disaster recovery procedures

While we implement security best practices, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We continuously update our security measures to protect your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request data deletion
  • Opt-out of data collection
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy team through the contact information provided below.
