Popular Topics

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Creating a CookIt account is easy:

  1. Download the CookIt app from App Store or Google Play
  2. Open the app and tap "Sign Up"
  3. Enter your email or continue with social media
  4. Complete your profile and preferences
How do I set up my kitchen preferences?

Customize your CookIt experience by:

  • Go to Settings > Kitchen Preferences
  • Select your dietary restrictions
  • Choose preferred cuisines
  • Set cooking skill level
  • Add kitchen equipment you own

Features Guide

How does ingredient recognition work?

CookIt's AI-powered ingredient recognition:

  • Open the camera in the app
  • Point at ingredients
  • AI automatically identifies items
  • Tap identified items to add to your inventory
  • Get recipe suggestions based on available ingredients
How do I use AR cooking guidance?

Follow these steps to use AR features:

  1. Select a recipe and tap "Start Cooking"
  2. Point your camera at your cooking space
  3. Follow the AR overlays for ingredient placement
  4. Get real-time guidance on techniques
  5. Use voice commands to navigate steps

Troubleshooting

Camera not working properly?

Try these solutions:

  1. Check camera permissions in device settings
  2. Ensure adequate lighting
  3. Clean your camera lens
  4. Restart the app
  5. Update to the latest version
AR features not loading?

Follow these steps:

  1. Check device compatibility
  2. Ensure stable internet connection
  3. Clear app cache
  4. Calibrate device sensors
  5. Contact support if issue persists
