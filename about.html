Our Story

Founded in 2023, CookIt emerged from a simple yet powerful idea: to make cooking accessible, enjoyable, and innovative for everyone. Our journey began when our founders, passionate about both technology and culinary arts, recognized the potential of AI to revolutionize the way we cook.

Our Mission

At CookIt, we're on a mission to transform every kitchen into a smart cooking space. We believe that everyone deserves access to the joy of cooking, regardless of their experience level.

Through innovative AI technology and augmented reality, we're breaking down the barriers that make cooking seem intimidating, while preserving the creativity and passion that makes it an art.

Our Values

Innovation

We constantly push the boundaries of what's possible in the kitchen, leveraging cutting-edge AI and AR technology to create new possibilities.

Community

We believe in the power of community and sharing. Our platform connects cooks worldwide, fostering a supportive environment for learning and growth.

Sustainability

We're committed to promoting sustainable cooking practices and helping users reduce food waste through smart ingredient management.
